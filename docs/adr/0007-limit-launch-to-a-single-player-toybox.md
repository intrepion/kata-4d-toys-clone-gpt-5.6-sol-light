# Limit launch to a single-player toybox

Elseplane will launch as a free, single-player, MIT-licensed experience without advertising, purchases, donations, premium gates, multiplayer, accounts, leaderboards, or networked Scene state. VR and mobile input, mesh export, a full Scene editor, community sharing, arbitrary user-authored 4D geometry, mods, cloud synchronization, and fully general 4D rigid-body physics are explicit future possibilities rather than incomplete launch work.
